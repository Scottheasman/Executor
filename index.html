Chelsea as Fiduciary holds their share of the house proceeds. She may apply income toward their support, maintenance, and education — but does not distribute principal until they reach age 30. No annual court accountings required (Will waives this).

Estate Attorney Fees — Monroe County

Hourly: Most Rochester estate attorneys charge $200–$500/hr. Simple estates run $1,500–$3,000 total.

Flat fee: Some offer $3,500+ for straightforward probate.

Who pays? Attorney fees come from the estate account — not your personal money.
