⚠️Risk
Medicaid Estate Recovery
Ask the attorney about this immediately
Ask First›

What Is It

If Elaine received Medicaid benefits at any point, New York State has a right to file a claim against the estate to recover those costs — potentially from the house sale proceeds.

What To Do

  • Ask the attorney at the first meeting: "Was Elaine on Medicaid?"
  • The McCM checklist specifically flags this — it's a real risk
  • If yes, NY OMIG (Office of Medicaid Inspector General) must be notified
  • This can delay or reduce the house sale distribution
Do not distribute house proceeds until the attorney confirms Medicaid recovery status is cleared.
